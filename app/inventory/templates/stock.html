Stock

Stock moves by itself: GRN puts goods in the QC Hold warehouse (Waiting for QC) · QC pass moves the passed quantity to the store (Available) · the rejected quantity stays in QC Hold (Rejected) until a Purchase Return sends it back · a Material issue moves RM from the store to WIP (In production) · a bill does not change stock. Values are estimated at the PO rate.
