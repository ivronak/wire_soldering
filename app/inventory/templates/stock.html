Stock

How much material you have, and where it is right now.

How does stock move? ▾

Goods received (GRN) → goes to QC Hold, shown as Waiting for QC.

QC passed → moves to the store, shown as Available.

QC rejected → stays in QC Hold as Rejected until a Purchase Return sends it back.

Material issue → store to production, shown as In production.

A bill never changes the quantity. Value is the PO rate (estimate) until the vendor bill is posted, then the bill rate.
